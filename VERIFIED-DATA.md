## Corporate Context

- **Todd Argall** became EVP & CEO on **November 13, 2019** (replacing Paul Roberts).
- Company originally founded as **Farm Bureau Mutual Insurance Company of Idaho (FBMIC)**.
- After Dec 2023 holding company restructuring, insurance subsidiary became **Farm Bureau Insurance Company of Idaho (FBICI)**.
- Holding company: **IFB Mutual Insurance Holding Company** (organized as a mutual holding company).
- Subsidiary: **WCIC** (Workers Compensation Insurance Company) — FBICI manages via 17%-of-premium service agreement.
- **Mountain West Farm Bureau Mutual Insurance Company** (est. 1948, Laramie WY) announced merger Nov 2024. Serves WY, MT, CO. Merger requires multi-state regulatory approval (~4 months). Mountain West converts from mutual to stock company owned by IFB Mutual.
